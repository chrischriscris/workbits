The core loop

A room, not a call.

The office is the product. Presence, distance, and a shared sense of place do the work that a grid of video tiles cannot.

◉

See the room

A warm isometric loft with a lounge, engineering pod, glass meeting room, kitchen, nap room, and garden. Click to walk; WASD if you prefer.

◒

Talk by distance

Voice fades with distance. Step into the meeting room and you move into a separate LiveKit room, so the conversation outside stays outside.

✦

Make it yours

Generate an avatar, tune the details, and rebuild the engineering pod with the Workspace Studio. Changes sync to everyone in the office.
