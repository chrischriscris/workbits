# Diorama acceptance checklist

Compare `artifacts/art/<run>/current.png` with `art/style-scene.png` at 1440×900, then again with the normal UI.

## Silhouette
- [ ] Whole plinth is visible with margin on all sides; no rooms cut off.
- [ ] Office reads as a contained cutaway, not a cropped floor plan.
- [ ] Base is a warm stone plinth; no water, lawn horizon, or floating grass slab.
- [ ] Only rear and left walls, clean cut ends, baseboards and pale caps.
- [ ] Meeting room reads as a glass box with charcoal frames and a door gap.

## Density and composition
- [ ] Meeting back-left: long table, 6 terracotta chairs, pendant, plants, art.
- [ ] Kitchen back-right: full cabinet run, sink, coffee machine, fridge, open shelves, mugs/jars, plants.
- [ ] Lounge left: sage sofa, cushions, coffee table, pouf, rug, shelf/console, floor lamp, large plant, art.
- [ ] Engineering center: joined 2×2 bench, privacy panels, four monitors, four chairs, plants, clutter.
- [ ] Garden and nap nooks on the right edge read as intentional rooms, not leftover floor.
- [ ] No large empty wood areas; every zone has layered props.
- [ ] All eight cast members are visible in sensible poses and don't overlap.

## Materials and light
- [ ] Light oak floor + pale tile zones; no flat single-color floor.
- [ ] Sage/terracotta/cream fabrics, charcoal metal, neutral glass, pale-blue screens.
- [ ] Warm key light + soft shadows + short-radius AO; no flat ambient-only look.
- [ ] Cream walls and plinth stay bright without clipping; oak/skin not muddy.
- [ ] Bloom only on lamps/screens; vignette subtle.

## Characters
- [ ] Heads ~40–45% of silhouette; large expressive eyes, brows, smile, ears.
- [ ] Hair reads as silhouette (curls, bun, fringe), not a helmet.
- [ ] Beards/glasses fit the face; no black blocks.
- [ ] Outfits and shoes have depth; hands/sleeves read.
- [ ] Team identities remain recognizable.

## UI
- [ ] Art mode hides all chrome and name tags.
- [ ] Normal UI keeps the 3D scene as the hero; no panel covers a key vignette.
- [ ] Desktop and mobile screenshots captured.
- [ ] No unexpected console errors (known R3F unmount warning allowed).
